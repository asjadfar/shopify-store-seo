# Collection Description Template — Shopify store SEO

> One copy per collection. Keep the description to 100–150 words. One
> main keyword in the opening line, once more in the body — never more.
> Fill in every [bracketed] field from the store's real facts. Never
> name a brand that is not in the collection, and never write a promise
> the store does not keep.

## The four-part structure

**1. Opening line — what the collection is and who it is for**

```
Shop [collection] at [Store Name] — [who it is for / what it is], [store offer in one phrase].
```

**2. What is inside — the brands or product types, named exactly as they appear in the store**

```
This collection includes [Brand 1], [Brand 2] and [Brand 3] — [one short phrase on the range, e.g. "fresh, woody and oriental fragrances"].
```

**3. Why buy here — one or two lines from the store's real offer**

```
[Delivery promise]. [Payment promise]. [Any other real offer, e.g. "Orders are dispatched within 24 hours."]
```

**4. Internal links — link to the two or three most relevant collections**

- A brand collection links to the audience collection it belongs to and to Bundles.
- An audience collection links to its top brand collections.
- A bundles collection links to the audience collections.

## Worked examples (from the reference store, Fragrance Empire)

### Audience collection — For Him

> Shop men's perfumes at Fragrance Empire — designer fragrances for him, delivered free across Malaysia. This collection includes Dior, Versace, Paco Rabanne and more — fresh, woody and oriental scents for day and evening wear. Free standard delivery in 3–5 business days, Cash on Delivery available, and orders are dispatched within 24 hours. Also see our [Dior perfumes](/collections/dior) and [perfume gift sets](/collections/bundles).

### Brand collection — Dior

> Shop Dior perfumes at Fragrance Empire — Sauvage, J'adore and more, delivered free across Malaysia. Fresh, woody and floral fragrances for men and women, in 100 ML bottles and gift sets. Free standard delivery in 3–5 business days, Cash on Delivery available. Browse more [perfumes for men](/collections/for-him), [perfumes for women](/collections/for-her), or save with a [perfume gift set](/collections/bundles).

### Bundles collection

> Shop perfume gift sets and bundles at Fragrance Empire — two or more designer fragrances in one set, at a lower price than buying them separately. Each set lists every perfume inside it by name and size, and the saving shown is the real saving on the set price. Free standard delivery in 3–5 business days, Cash on Delivery available. Also browse [perfumes for him](/collections/for-him) and [perfumes for her](/collections/for-her).
