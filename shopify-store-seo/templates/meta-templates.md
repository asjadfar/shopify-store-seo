# Meta Title / Description Templates — Shopify store SEO

> Character limits are hard limits — count before publishing. Use the
> checker (`scripts/check_seo.py`) to catch over-long or missing meta
> tags. Every meta title and meta description must be unique across the
> store. Fill in every [bracketed] field from the owner's real product
> facts — never invent a brand, size, note or claim.
>
> Limits: meta title up to 60 characters; meta description 120–160
> characters; product title up to 70 characters.

## Product

**Meta title pattern**

```
[Brand] [Product Name] [Type] [Size] | [Store Name]
```

- Drop the store name first if the title runs long.
- Example (44 characters): `Dior Sauvage EDP 100 ML | Fragrance Empire`

**Meta description pattern**

```
Buy [Brand] [Product Name] [Type] [Size] online in [Market]. [One benefit phrase]. [Store offer] at [Store Name].
```

- Example: `Buy Dior Sauvage EDP 100 ML online in Malaysia. Fresh woody fragrance for men. Free delivery, Cash on Delivery available at Fragrance Empire.`
- One keyword, one benefit, one offer. Never repeat the keyword more than twice, and never write a claim the store cannot keep.

**Bundle meta title pattern**

```
[Bundle Name] — [Count] Perfumes | [Store Name]
```

- Example: `His & Her Gift Set — 2 Perfumes | Fragrance Empire`

**Bundle meta description pattern**

```
[Bundle Name]: [Product 1] + [Product 2] in one set. Save [Amount] [Currency]. [Store offer] at [Store Name].
```

- The saving must match the actual bundle prices. State it in the same currency as the prices.

## Collection

**Meta title pattern**

```
[Collection Name] | [Store Name]
```

- Audience collection example: `Perfume for Men | Fragrance Empire`
- Brand collection example: `Dior Perfumes | Fragrance Empire`
- Bundles example: `Perfume Gift Sets & Bundles | Fragrance Empire`

**Meta description pattern**

```
Shop [collection description in 5–8 words] online in [Market]. [Store offer].
```

- Audience example: `Shop men's perfumes online in Malaysia — designer fragrances for him. Free delivery in 3–5 days, Cash on Delivery available.`
- Brand example: `Shop Dior perfumes online in Malaysia — Sauvage, J'adore and more. Free delivery in 3–5 days, Cash on Delivery available.`
- Bundles example: `Shop perfume gift sets and bundles online in Malaysia — two or more fragrances in one set. Free delivery, Cash on Delivery available.`
- Name only brands that are actually in the collection.

## Homepage

**Meta title pattern**

```
[Store Name] — [main keyword phrase]
```

- Example: `Fragrance Empire — Buy Perfume Online in Malaysia`

**Meta description pattern**

```
[Store Name] — [what the store sells] in [Market]. [Number] brands, [store offer].
```

- Example: `Fragrance Empire — designer perfumes online in Malaysia. 18 brands, free delivery in 3–5 days, Cash on Delivery available.`

## Store pages

**Meta title pattern**

```
[Page Name] | [Store Name]
```

- Examples: `About Us | Fragrance Empire`, `Contact Us | Fragrance Empire`, `Shipping Policy | Fragrance Empire`

**Meta description pattern**

```
[One sentence saying what the page is for]. [Store Name] — [store offer, if relevant].
```

- About example: `About Fragrance Empire — designer perfumes online in Malaysia, delivered free in 3–5 days.`
- Contact example: `Contact Fragrance Empire — email, phone and WhatsApp for orders, delivery and returns.`
- Shipping example: `Fragrance Empire shipping — free standard delivery across Malaysia in 3–5 business days.`
